# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, clear, and constructive.
- Respect different experiences and points of view.
- Give and accept useful feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Keep private information private.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, and
publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to `support@hackathon.new`. The maintainers
will review reports fairly and may edit or remove content, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently ban participation. Reporters and subjects will be
treated with care and confidentiality where possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project in public spaces.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1,
available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
